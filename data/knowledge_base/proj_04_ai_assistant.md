### Client Problem
A B2B software vendor faced a backlog of 300+ customer support tickets daily, with an average first-response time exceeding 6 hours.

### Solution Delivered
Built a multi-step agentic triaging pipeline with LangChain and FastAPI that parsed inbound Zendesk tickets, retrieved relevant technical documentation from Chroma, drafted suggested resolutions, and categorized priority level.

### Tangible Outcome
Automated the resolution of 42% of tier-1 support tickets and reduced average first-response time to under 12 minutes.
